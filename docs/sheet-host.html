Loop Network is a local network of TVs in the busiest bars, gyms, and shops in town. Each screen plays a clean loop of 15-second local ads. Put one on your wall and your own business runs on every screen in the network, at no cost to you.

What is Loop Network?

Advertisers pay to reach local customers on our screens. They build campaigns by spending points on the venues they want to appear in. Your location is part of that network, so the busier and more visible your spot is, the more valuable your screen becomes. You host the screen, and in return you advertise free across the whole network.
